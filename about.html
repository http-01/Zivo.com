About Us

The story behind ZIVO

We built ZIVO for people who don't just want to know that someone is famous — but why.

ZIVO was created for the endlessly curious. Headlines tell you what someone just did; they rarely tell you how they got there. We wanted a place that goes further back — to the classrooms, garages, street corners, and small stages where every famous story actually begins.

Every profile on ZIVO is organized the same way: Early Life, Education, Career, Achievements, and Interesting Facts, with a Net Worth section included only when a specific, reputable source has reported a figure. We'd rather leave a section out than fill it with a guess.

ZIVO covers five worlds — Actors, Singers, Athletes, Creators, and Entrepreneurs — because fame today comes from a film set, a stadium, a studio, a stream, or a startup, equally.

What We Stand For

Our Values

Accuracy & Care

We favor leaving a detail out over guessing at one. Where public information is unreliable or unavailable, we say so.

Original Storytelling

Every biography is researched from public sources and written in our own words.

Global Perspective

Fame isn't limited to one country or industry. We catalogue icons from every continent and craft.

Respect for Privacy

Even public figures deserve boundaries. We focus on public, career-relevant milestones — not personal intrusion.
