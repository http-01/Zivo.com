PewDiePie

Content Creator

01

Overview

A university dropout who once forgot his own YouTube password became the individual creator credited with popularizing the gaming "Let's Play" format.

02

Early Life

Felix Arvid Ulf Kjellberg was born in Gothenburg, Sweden, to parents who both worked as company executives. As a child he was drawn to art and video games in roughly equal measure, often drawing characters like Mario and Sonic the Hedgehog, and he sold some of his artwork as a teenager to help buy his first computer.

03

Education

Kjellberg enrolled at Chalmers University of Technology in Gothenburg to study Industrial Economics and Technology Management, but left before completing his degree to pursue content creation full time.

04

Career

Kjellberg created a first YouTube channel in 2006 but lost access to the account; he returned in 2010 under the name PewDiePie, initially posting horror and action game commentary in the "Let's Play" style. The channel's energetic, unscripted humor set it apart, and it became the most-subscribed channel on YouTube in August 2013, a position it held for years.

His content later broadened into comedy sketches, reaction videos, and long-running series reviewing content submitted by fans, alongside a return to gaming through Minecraft. Beyond YouTube, he has released video games, a parody self-help book, and continues to be cited as one of the defining figures in the platform's early gaming culture.

05

Achievements

  • Became the first individual YouTube channel to reach 50 million subscribers
  • Held the title of most-subscribed YouTube channel for several years
  • Named to Time magazine's 100 Most Influential People list (2016)
  • Voted Most Popular Gaming Web Star at the Teen Choice Awards (2014)
  • Raised funds for charities including the World Wildlife Fund and St. Jude Children's Research Hospital
06

Interesting Facts

  • His channel name references the sound of a laser gun ("pew")
  • Left Chalmers University of Technology to pursue YouTube full time
  • Released a parody self-help book, This Book Loves You, in 2015
  • Married fellow content creator Marzia Bisognin, whom he originally met online, in 2019
  • Created and released two video games based on his channel
