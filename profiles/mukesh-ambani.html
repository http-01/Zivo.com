Mukesh Ambani

Chairman & Managing Director, Reliance Industries · India

Mukesh Ambani is an Indian business magnate and the Chairman and Managing Director of Reliance Industries Limited, one of India's largest conglomerates.

Last updated: 21 August 2026

Biography

Mukesh Ambani was born on 19 April 1957 in Aden, then part of the British Crown Colony of Aden (present-day Yemen), to Dhirubhai Ambani, the founder of Reliance Industries.

He studied chemical engineering at the Institute of Chemical Technology (then UDCT) in Mumbai and later enrolled in the MBA program at Stanford University, which he left to join the family business.

Following his father's death in 2002, Mukesh Ambani took over as Chairman and Managing Director of Reliance Industries, overseeing its expansion into petrochemicals, refining, oil, telecommunications (Jio) and retail.

Career

  • Joined Reliance Industries and helped build its petrochemicals and refining businesses
  • Became Chairman & Managing Director of Reliance Industries in 2002
  • Led the launch of Reliance Jio, a telecommunications operator, in 2016
  • Oversees Reliance Retail, one of India's largest retail businesses
