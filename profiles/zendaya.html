Zendaya

Actress & Producer

01

Overview

A Disney Channel dance-troupe teenager who became the youngest two-time Emmy winner in her category.

02

Early Life

Zendaya Maree Stoermer Coleman was born in Oakland, California, to Claire Stoermer and Kazembe Ajamu Coleman, both of whom worked as teachers; her name means “to give thanks” in the Shona language. As a child she trained with the hip-hop dance troupe Future Shock Oakland and practiced hula with the Academy of Hawaiian Arts.

She also took part in programs at the California Shakespeare Theater and the Oakland School for the Arts, and began working as a child fashion model for retailers including Macy's and Old Navy before her acting career began.

03

Education

Zendaya's early training came through community arts programs rather than a conventional school path — including the Oakland School for the Arts and California Shakespeare Theater — as her family moved toward Los Angeles once she began pursuing acting professionally in middle school.

04

Career

Zendaya's career took off at thirteen when she was cast as Rocky Blue in the Disney Channel series Shake It Up (2010–2013), and she later competed on Dancing with the Stars as its youngest-ever contestant. She made her film breakthrough in 2017, playing MJ in Spider-Man: Homecoming and appearing in The Greatest Showman.

Her role as Rue Bennett in the HBO series Euphoria, beginning in 2019, marked a turn toward more complex material and earned her two Primetime Emmy Awards for outstanding lead actress (2020 and 2022) — making her the youngest woman to win the category and, in 2022, the first Black woman to win it twice. She has since starred in the science-fiction epic Dune (2021, 2024) and the tennis drama Challengers (2024).

05

Achievements

  • Youngest actress to win the Primetime Emmy for Outstanding Lead Actress in a Drama Series
  • First Black woman to win that Emmy category twice
  • Won a Golden Globe Award for best actress in a television drama (2023)
  • Named to Time magazine's 100 Most Influential People list (2022)
  • Films she has starred in have grossed billions of dollars worldwide
06

Interesting Facts

  • Her name means “to give thanks” in the Shona language
  • Was the youngest-ever contestant on Dancing with the Stars, finishing as runner-up
  • Launched her own clothing line, Daya by Zendaya
  • Serves as an ambassador for the charity Convoy of Hope
  • Also pursued a music career alongside acting as a teenager
