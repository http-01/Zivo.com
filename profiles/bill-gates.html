Bill Gates

Entrepreneur, Philanthropist & Author

01

Overview

A Harvard dropout who co-founded Microsoft went on to build the world's largest private philanthropic foundation.

02

Early Life

William Henry Gates III was born in Seattle, Washington, the son of a lawyer and a schoolteacher and community volunteer. Known to his family as "Trey," he was a competitive, avid reader from an early age, and has said he read through an entire encyclopedia set as a child.

He began programming as a teenager, writing his first program, a game of tic-tac-toe, at thirteen, and later helped computerize his high school's class-scheduling and payroll systems.

03

Education

Gates enrolled at Harvard University in 1973 but left before graduating, in his junior year, to pursue a software venture with his childhood friend Paul Allen full time.

04

Career

Gates and Allen founded Microsoft in 1975. The company's defining early moment came in 1980, when Gates licensed the MS-DOS operating system to IBM for its first personal computer, a deal that positioned Microsoft software at the center of the emerging PC industry. Microsoft released Windows in 1985, and by the 1990s its software ran the large majority of personal computers worldwide.

Gates served as Microsoft's CEO and later chairman for decades, becoming the company's largest shareholder and, for a period, the world's richest private individual. He stepped back from day-to-day management in 2008 to focus on the Bill & Melinda Gates Foundation, established in 2000, which has since become the largest private charitable foundation in the United States, focused mainly on global health and education.

05

Achievements

  • Co-founded Microsoft Corporation, which became the world's largest personal-computer software company
  • Co-founded the Gates Foundation, the largest private charitable foundation in the United States
  • Co-launched the Giving Pledge with Warren Buffett, encouraging billionaires to commit the majority of their wealth to charity
  • Received the Presidential Medal of Freedom in 2016
  • Published multiple books, including The Road Ahead and the memoir Source Code: My Beginnings
06

Interesting Facts

  • Began reading encyclopedias for fun at age eight
  • Wrote his first computer program, a tic-tac-toe game, at thirteen
  • Pledged in 2025 to give away 99% of his remaining fortune to the Gates Foundation
  • Helped introduce the Xbox gaming console during his later years at Microsoft
  • Has three children with former wife Melinda French Gates
