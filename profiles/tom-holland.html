Tom Holland

01

Overview

A West End dance prodigy who became the youngest actor ever to headline a Marvel title role.

02

Early Life

Thomas Stanley Holland was born in Kingston upon Thames, London, the eldest of four sons of photographer Nicola Frost and comedian-author Dominic Holland. He was diagnosed with dyslexia as a child, something he has since spoken about publicly.

He started hip-hop dance classes at nine, largely to keep up with his younger brothers, and a choreographer who spotted him at a local class recommended him for a youth theatre audition soon after.

03

Education

Holland attended Donhead Preparatory School and Wimbledon College before training at the BRIT School for Performing Arts and Technology, a London school known for producing a number of working British actors and musicians.

04

Career

At twelve, Holland was cast in the title role of the West End production of Billy Elliot the Musical, performing more than a hundred shows over nearly two years. His film debut came in 2012's tsunami drama The Impossible, which earned him a National Board of Review award for breakthrough performance.

Marvel Studios signed Holland to a multi-picture deal in 2015, and he debuted as Peter Parker in Captain America: Civil War the following year, becoming the youngest actor to headline a title role in the Marvel Cinematic Universe. He has since starred in three solo Spider-Man films and multiple Avengers ensemble movies, alongside other lead roles including Nathan Drake in Uncharted and starring parts in The Devil All the Time and Cherry.

05

Achievements

  • Youngest actor to headline a title role in the Marvel Cinematic Universe
  • Won a BAFTA Rising Star Award and three Saturn Awards
  • Films he has starred in have grossed a combined total of more than $9 billion worldwide
  • Named to the Forbes 30 Under 30 Europe list
  • Returned to the London stage in a 2024 West End revival of Romeo and Juliet
06

Interesting Facts

  • Has spoken publicly about living with dyslexia since childhood
  • Trained as a competitive gymnast and dancer before acting
  • Founded the production company Billy17 in 2024
  • A lifelong supporter of Tottenham Hotspur football club
  • His parents founded The Brothers Trust, a charity supporting a range of causes
