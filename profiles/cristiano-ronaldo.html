Cristiano Ronaldo

Professional Footballer (Forward)

01

Overview

A kit man's son from Madeira who became the first men's footballer to reach 900 career goals.

02

Early Life

Cristiano Ronaldo dos Santos Aveiro was born in Funchal on the Portuguese island of Madeira, the youngest of four children. His father worked as an equipment manager for local club Andorinha, where Ronaldo began playing as a boy.

At fifteen, he underwent heart surgery to correct a racing-heart condition that could have ended his career before it began; the surgery was successful and he returned to football soon after.

03

Education

Ronaldo moved away from his family in Madeira as a young teenager to join Sporting Clube de Portugal's youth academy in Lisbon, where football training took priority over a conventional secondary education.

04

Career

After progressing through Sporting CP's academy, Ronaldo made his first-team debut in 2002 and was signed by Manchester United a year later for a fee that set an English record for a teenager at the time. His breakout season came in 2007–08, when he won the Premier League, the Champions League, and his first Ballon d'Or.

Ronaldo went on to play for Real Madrid, where he became the club's all-time leading scorer, and later Juventus, before returning to Manchester United and then joining Saudi Arabian club Al Nassr. Across his club and international career he has won five Ballon d'Or awards, become the first men's player to reach 900 official career goals, and captained Portugal to victory at the 2016 European Championship.

05

Achievements

  • Won five Ballon d'Or awards (2008, 2013, 2014, 2016, 2017)
  • First men's player to score 900 official career goals
  • Portugal's all-time leading goal scorer and most-capped player
  • Captained Portugal to its first major title at the 2016 UEFA European Championship
  • Holds the men's record for scoring across the most different FIFA World Cup tournaments
06

Interesting Facts

  • Was named after former U.S. President Ronald Reagan, his father's favorite actor
  • Has worn four different squad numbers across his career: 28, 17, 9, and 7
  • Represented Portugal at the 2004 Athens Olympics
  • Became the oldest outfield player to start a World Cup match, at the 2026 tournament
  • His first professional appearance came against Braga in September 2002
