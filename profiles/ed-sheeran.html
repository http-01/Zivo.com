Ed Sheeran

Singer-Songwriter

01

Overview

A stuttering child who found his voice through Eminem lyrics grew up to sell more than 150 million records.

02

Early Life

Edward Christopher Sheeran was born in Halifax, West Yorkshire, and raised in Framlingham, Suffolk, the son of an art curator father and a jewelry-designer mother, with an older brother who became a composer. He began singing in his church choir at four and started learning guitar as a child.

As a boy Sheeran had a pronounced stutter; he has said that memorizing a fast-paced Eminem record around age ten helped him work through it. He met singer-songwriter Damien Rice backstage at a concert around age eleven, an encounter he has credited with pushing him toward songwriting.

03

Education

Sheeran attended Thomas Mills High School in Framlingham until he was sixteen, when he left to pursue music full time, having already spent his mid-teens travelling to open-mic nights and self-releasing recordings.

04

Career

Sheeran spent several years performing at pubs and clubs across the UK, at one point playing more than 300 gigs in a single year, and self-released a string of independent EPs before a viral 2010 performance and the EP No. 5 Collaborations Project led to a deal with Atlantic Records. His debut album, + (2011), reached the top five of the Billboard 200.

Subsequent albums x (2014) and ÷ (2017) turned Sheeran into one of the best-selling artists of his generation, with singles "Shape of You" and "Perfect" both reaching number one on the Billboard Hot 100. His Divide Tour became one of the highest-grossing concert tours ever, and he has continued releasing new material, including 2025's Play.

05

Achievements

  • Sold more than 150 million records, among the best-selling music artists in history
  • "Shape of You" and "Perfect" both reached number one on the Billboard Hot 100
  • The Divide Tour ranked among the highest-grossing and most-attended concert tours ever
  • Won multiple Grammy Awards
  • Has written hit songs recorded by other major artists, including Justin Bieber and Taylor Swift
06

Interesting Facts

  • Overcame a childhood stutter partly by memorizing a fast-tempo Eminem album
  • Left school at sixteen to pursue music full time
  • Founded the Ed Sheeran Suffolk Music Foundation to support young musicians
  • Has acted in small roles in Game of Thrones and Bridget Jones's Baby
  • Married Cherry Seaborn, a friend from his school days
