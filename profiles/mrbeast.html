MrBeast

01

Overview

A teenager posting videos under an accidental Xbox gamertag became the most-subscribed individual creator on YouTube.

02

Early Life

James Stephen Donaldson was born in Wichita, Kansas, and raised mainly by his mother in Greenville, North Carolina, with the family relocating frequently because of her career. He was the second of three children.

He uploaded his first YouTube video in February 2012 at age thirteen, adopting the channel name "MrBeast6000" from a gamertag his Xbox had randomly assigned him, later shortened to MrBeast as the channel grew.

03

Education

Donaldson has said he spent more of his teenage years studying YouTube's algorithm and analyzing other creators' channels than on traditional schoolwork, choosing to commit himself to the platform full time rather than pursue higher education.

04

Career

Donaldson's early videos were gaming Let's Plays and commentary estimating other YouTubers' earnings. His channel's turning point came when he shifted toward large-scale challenge and stunt videos, and later toward elaborate philanthropic giveaways, a format that set his channel apart and drove rapid subscriber growth.

His channel became the first to surpass 300 million subscribers in 2024 and later passed 500 million. Beyond YouTube, Donaldson has built a wider business under Beast Industries, including the snack brand Feastables and the Amazon Prime Video competition series Beast Games, while also co-founding large-scale charitable campaigns such as Team Trees and Team Seas.

05

Achievements

  • Most-subscribed individual content creator on YouTube
  • Won the Streamy Award for Creator of the Year three times (2020, 2021, 2022)
  • Co-created Team Trees and Team Seas, environmental fundraising campaigns that together raised tens of millions of dollars
  • Holds the Guinness World Record for the largest donation total in a single livestream
  • Beast Games became the most-watched unscripted series premiere in Amazon Prime Video's history
06

Interesting Facts

  • His channel name began as a randomly generated Xbox gamertag, "MrBeast6000"
  • Has stated he reinvests the large majority of his earnings back into producing videos
  • Co-founded Feastables, a chocolate and snack brand
  • Received the YouTube Diamond Play Button, awarded only to channels that pass 100 million subscribers
  • Married content creator Thea Booysen in 2026
