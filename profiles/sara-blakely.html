01

Overview

A door-to-door fax machine salesperson cut the feet off a pair of pantyhose and built the idea into a billion-dollar company.

02

Early Life

Sara Treleaven Blakely grew up in Clearwater, Florida. Her father would ask the family at dinner what they had each failed at that week, a ritual she has credited with teaching her to treat effort, not just success, as worth celebrating — a mindset she later leaned on through more than twenty manufacturer rejections while developing her first product.

03

Education

Blakely earned a bachelor's degree in Communications from Florida State University, then worked briefly at Walt Disney World before becoming a salesperson and later a national sales trainer at the office-supply company Danka, where she sold fax machines door to door.

04

Career

Frustrated by visible pantyhose seams under a pair of white trousers, Blakely cut the feet off a pair of pantyhose to wear under open-toed shoes, and spent two years and $5,000 in personal savings developing the idea into a product, teaching herself to write her own patent application using library books to avoid legal fees. She founded Spanx in 2000 after Neiman Marcus placed the company's first major order.

A feature on Oprah Winfrey's "favorite things" list drove early sales, and by 2012 Spanx was valued at $1 billion with Blakely as its sole owner, making her Forbes' youngest self-made female billionaire. She later brought in the investment firm Blackstone as a majority partner in a 2021 deal valuing the company at $1.2 billion, and in 2024 launched a footwear brand, Sneex.

05

Achievements

  • Became the world's youngest self-made female billionaire in 2012, per Forbes
  • Built Spanx from $5,000 in savings into a company valued above $1 billion without traditional advertising
  • First woman to join the Giving Pledge, committing the majority of her wealth to charity
  • Named to Time magazine's 100 Most Influential People list (2012)
  • Founded the Sara Blakely Foundation, supporting women's entrepreneurship and education
06

Interesting Facts

  • Invented Spanx by cutting the feet off a pair of pantyhose
  • Wrote her own patent application using library books to save on legal fees
  • Appeared as a contestant on Richard Branson's reality show The Rebel Billionaire
  • Is a minority owner of the NBA's Atlanta Hawks
  • Launched a comfort-focused high-heel footwear brand, Sneex, in 2024
